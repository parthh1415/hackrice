Firebreak reverse-stress-tests your portfolio, models how crowded institutional selling amplifies losses, and finds the smallest change that makes it more resilient.

What loss would you refuse to tolerate?

Advanced assumptions

Gross leverage, price impact and the breach band describe the institutional network around your holdings, not your account. They live on the Risk Desk rail and default to 5.0×, 0.20 and 1.05.
